ASIA TRAVEL GUIDE & PRICING CONTEXT

1. Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka):
- Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY).
- Budget expectations: Mid-range hotel: 12,000 to 25,000 JPY per night (~7,000 to 14,000 INR / $80 to $160 USD). Ramen meal: 900 to 1,500 JPY (~500 to 900 INR). Temple admissions: 500 to 1,000 JPY (~300 to 600 INR).
- Highlights: Senso-ji Temple, Shibuya Crossing, Fushimi Inari Shrine, Mount Fuji day tour, Dotonbori street food.
- Transport: Shinkansen (Bullet Train), IC Cards (Suica, Pasmo).

2. Thailand (Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai):
- Currency: Thai Baht (THB).
- Budget expectations: Hotel per night: 1,200 to 3,500 THB (~3,000 to 8,000 INR). Street food: 60 to 150 THB (~150 to 350 INR).
- Highlights: Grand Palace, Wat Arun, Island Hopping in Phuket, Elephant Sanctuaries in Chiang Mai.
