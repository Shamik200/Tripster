GENERAL TRAVEL TIPS & PACKING GUIDELINES

1. Money & Currency:
- Always carry a combination of credit cards (without foreign transaction fees) and local cash.
- Research local tipping etiquette before arrival. In Western countries (USA, UK), 15-20% is expected, whereas in Japan tipping is considered disrespectful.
- When paying by card abroad, always choose to pay in the LOCAL currency rather than your home currency to get the best exchange rate from your bank.

2. Health & Safety:
- Keep digital and physical copies of essential travel documents, including passport, visa, insurance policy, and vaccination cards.
- Drink bottled or filtered water in areas where tap water quality is uncertain.
- Store emergency contact numbers and local embassy information on your phone.

3. Logistics & Transit:
- Download offline maps (e.g. Google Maps or Maps.me) before arriving at your destination.
- Research public transport passes (like Suica/Pasmo in Japan, Oyster in London, or Deutschlandticket in Germany) for major savings.
- Arrive at international airports at least 3 hours prior to departure.
