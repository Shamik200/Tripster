USA TRAVEL GUIDE & PRICING CONTEXT

1. New York City, New York:
- Currency: US Dollar (USD).
- Budget expectations: Hotel per night: $180 to $350 USD (~15,000 to 29,000 INR). Meals: $20 to $70 USD per person. Broadway shows: $80 to $200 USD.
- Highlights: Statue of Liberty, Times Square, Central Park, Empire State Building, Metropolitan Museum of Art.

2. San Francisco & California:
- Currency: US Dollar (USD).
- Highlights: Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz Island, Fisherman's Wharf, Yosemite National Park.
