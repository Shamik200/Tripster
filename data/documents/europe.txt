EUROPE TRAVEL GUIDE & PRICING CONTEXT

1. Germany (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt):
- Currency: Euro (EUR). 
- Budget expectations: Mid-range hotel: 80 to 180 EUR per night (~7,000 to 16,000 INR). Fine dining: 50 to 120 EUR per person (~4,500 to 11,000 INR). Museum entries: 10 to 20 EUR (~900 to 1,800 INR).
- Highlights: Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island, Neuschwanstein Castle, Oktoberfest, Munich Marienplatz.
- Transport: DB (Deutsche Bahn) train network, S-Bahn, U-Bahn.

2. France (Paris, Nice, Lyon):
- Currency: Euro (EUR).
- Budget expectations: Hotel per night: 100 to 250 EUR. Eiffel Tower tickets: 25 to 35 EUR. Louvre entry: 22 EUR. Dinner: 35 to 90 EUR.
- Highlights: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Palace of Versailles, French Riviera beaches.

3. Italy (Rome, Florence, Venice):
- Currency: Euro (EUR).
- Highlights: Colosseum, Vatican Museums, Florence Uffizi Gallery, Gondola in Venice.
- City tax (Tassa di Soggiorno) is paid in cash at hotel check-in (approx 3 to 7 EUR per person per night).
